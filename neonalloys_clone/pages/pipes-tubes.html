    Stainless & Duplex Steel Pipes & Tubes

    Leveraging on our finest manufacturing unit and experienced work force, our company offers stainless and duplex steel pipes. High quality pipes are stainless because of a protective layer on their surfaces which reduces the rate of corrosion to almost negligible levels. Available in different grades and dimensions, these stainless steel and duplex steel pipes are widely used invarious industries suchasconstruction. cement. petrochemical and more.
