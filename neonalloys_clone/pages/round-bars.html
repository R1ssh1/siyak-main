Round Bars & Rods

We are offering to our clients a wide range of Round Bars that are available in varied grades of stainless steel, carbon steel, nickel alloys and alloy steel. Our Round Bars have great utility as machinery equipment in various industries and for diverse architectural purposes. These round bars feature superior polish, excellent finish, sturdy construction and high tolerance level.
