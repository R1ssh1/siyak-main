Flanges

Fasteners

We design quality fasteners for critical machine applications and desired results.

Forms: Stud Bolts, Hex Bolts, Square Bolts, Hex Nuts, T-Head Bolts, Structural Bolts, U-Bolts, Foundation Bolts, As per drawing and customers specifications.
Ferrule Fittings

We are engaged in importing and supplying a wide range of Ferrule Fittings that is available in different grades and specifications.

Material Of Construction : 304,304L,316, 316L etc Finish : Mechanical Polish as well as Electropolish finishes.
Valves

Buttweld Fittings
